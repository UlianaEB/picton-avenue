This is the recommended structure. An NZLP is tax-transparent — each LP partner pays tax on their share of income at their own marginal rate. The GP manages the asset and earns fees and a promote. This is precisely how Mackersy Property, First Light Capital, and most NZ commercial property syndicates operate for wholesale investors. You can close within your timeline using a conditional S&P and an Information Memorandum instead of a full PDS.

Recommended pathway — given the deadline

1. Sign a conditional S&P today

Include a 30-day due diligence condition and a finance condition. This secures the deal without needing LP capital committed yet. A conditional agreement is standard and protects you while you raise equity.

2. Engage a commercial solicitor this week

Brief them on the NZLP structure. They will draft the LP Agreement, GP entity documents, and Information Memorandum. Budget $8,000–$15,000. Also confirm the Arise heads of agreement is properly documented.

3. Approach 6–10 wholesale-qualified investors privately

Target individuals who qualify as wholesale investors under the FMCA. No public advertising. Circulate the IM directly. Aim to have commitments in hand before going unconditional on the S&P. $50k–$100k per LP unit is the right range for this deal size.

4. Complete AML/CFT and register the LP

Every investor must be verified under the AML/CFT Act 2009 — identity, address and source of wealth. File the LP with the Companies Office. This runs in parallel with the equity raise. Do not accept funds before verification is complete.

5. Settle and begin asset management

Arrange the bank loan (LTC facility at 35% equity), execute the LP capital calls, settle the purchase. Begin roof and refurbishment capex. Negotiate the Arise net lease HOA into a formal agreement within the first 60 days of ownership.
